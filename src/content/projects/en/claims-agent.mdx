## The problem

At an insurer, every decision on a claim has to be justified: to the policyholder, to an auditor, sometimes to a judge. An agent that "reasons" in one block cannot do that: you see the answer, not the path.

## The approach

The business process becomes a graph. Each step takes and returns typed data, checked against a schema, so every decision can be replayed and verified.

Steps that follow rules (deadlines, ceilings, mandatory documents) are code. Only the steps that require reading and interpreting (classifying a document, analyzing circumstances, drafting a letter) go to a model, and code checks their output before moving on.

## The demo

Change the claim: remove a document, extend the delay, switch the circumstances. The path changes, and the audit log shows why.

## What the demo simplifies

The claim and the policy are fictional. The outputs of the model steps are precomputed: no language model runs in your browser. The real system was tested against the actual business APIs.
